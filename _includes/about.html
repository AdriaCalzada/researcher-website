About

Connecting atomic-scale insight with real-world performance.

I am a computational chemistry researcher and PhD candidate in Nanoscience at the University of Barcelona's Institute of Theoretical and Computational Chemistry (IQTC).

My work combines density functional theory and molecular dynamics to study atomically engineered two-dimensional carbon membranes, particularly grazynes. I focus on adsorption, diffusion and selectivity in processes including CO2 capture, air purification, biogas upgrading and syngas control.

A research stay with the Alexandrova Lab at UCLA expanded my experience in complex catalytic environments and global optimization.
