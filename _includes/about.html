About

Computational chemistry · DFT/MD · 2D membranes

I’m Adrià Calzada—a Chemistry graduate turned computational researcher, currently doing a PhD in Nanoscience (Chemistry) at the University of Barcelona (IQTC). I use DFT and molecular dynamics to design and understand atomically engineered 2D carbon membranes (grazynes) for selective gas separation, with a particular focus on CO2 capture and clean-energy applications. I like connecting the “atomic story” (adsorption, diffusion, selectivity) to performance you can actually compare, and I’ve published this work in venues like ChemSusChem, Nanomaterials, and the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy. I also spent a research stay at UCLA (Alexandrova Group) sharpening my workflow for tackling complex catalytic environments and global optimization—this site is where I share the projects, papers, and tools that come out of that journey.
